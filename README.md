I build fast, polished products and ship them to production. Over **6+ years** I've shipped **6+ production apps** for startups in fintech, payments, escrow, e-commerce, education and religious tech, reaching **10,000+ active users**. As **CTO at Borderwave** I led the whole stack: 2 mobile apps, 1 backend, 4 core services and invoice payments across **5 countries** at zero downtime.

Right now I'm focused on **Shopify development**: connecting stores to the rest of a business with API integrations, inventory sync bots and custom apps.
